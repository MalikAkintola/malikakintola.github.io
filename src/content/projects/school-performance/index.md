## The question

Students were doing badly in final exams, and the school only found out when the results came in. The
report gives the school a live view of performance during the year, and an early warning for each
student while there is still time to help.

## A view for each person

The principal’s view covers the whole school: 400 students, 51 staff, a teacher to student ratio of
1 to 10 and an average pass rate of 62.55%, with students broken down by gender, class, department and
term. Each teacher gets a view of their own subject, with every student’s first and second term scores
and the pass rate for each class.

## The early warning

The predictions page lists every student with their library hours, attendance and first and second
term scores, next to a predicted third-term score. Colour runs from red to blue, so the students who
need attention stand out first. A search box and class filter find any student quickly.
